## Illustrative party flags (SVG)

All other `*.svg` files in this folder are **original illustrations** drawn for this dashboard. They follow publicly described party colours and simple electoral motifs (broom, umbrella, cock, etc.) so the UI shows flag-like marks instead of colour-initial placeholders.

They are **not** official party trademarks or scanned campaign flags. Trademarked APC / PDP / LP / NNPP / APGA / ADC (and smaller-party) wordmarks and photographic logos are generally not available under a free license suitable for local hosting; INEC historical `inec/pix/*.jpg` URLs are no longer live.

### Gaps / follow-ups

- Prefer replacing major-party SVGs with INEC-published or party-permitted flag/logo files when a redistributable source appears.
- Historic Third Republic SDP logo exists on Commons as [Nigerian_SDP_logo.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nigerian_SDP_logo.png) (CC BY 4.0) but depicts the defunct Third Republic party; the dashboard keeps the contemporary SDP illustration instead.
